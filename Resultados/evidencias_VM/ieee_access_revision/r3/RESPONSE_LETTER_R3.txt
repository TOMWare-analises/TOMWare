a) Concern: clarify implications of the ~29% re-execution rate and selection bias.
b) Response: Agreed. In the earlier corpus protocol, additional attempts were allowed after

   Retaining only eventually threshold-satisfying runs can overstate success and bias timing
   summaries. Mitigation: (1) explicit first-attempt statistics; (2) descriptive framing for
   corpus aggregates; (3) redesigned SkewMask oracle with fixed N and all runs retained.
