Step 0 — Prepare
-----------------
  cd C:\TOMWare\m3_v12_scripts   (or the folder where you pasted the script)
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Run-AccessR43.ps1 -Job Prepare
Expected: DLL 4BBC6270, 3 x OK. If MISSING, copy the PEs and Prepare again.
